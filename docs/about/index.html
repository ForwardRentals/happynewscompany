About Happy News Company

Happy News Company publishes real good news: medical breakthroughs, wildlife comebacks, clean energy wins, generous communities and businesses doing good. Every story is true, sourced and written to leave you a little more hopeful than you were.

We do not invent feel-good stories or exaggerate them. Good news is more powerful when you can trust it.

Every news story links to its primary source or the publication that first reported it. When a story is based on reports rather than an official announcement, we say so in the headline or the first paragraph.

Press releases

We also publish press releases from companies, nonprofits and community groups sharing their own good news. Releases are clearly labeled, kept separate from our news coverage, and never influence what our newsdesk covers. See packages and pricing.

Corrections

If we got something wrong, tell us through our contact page and we will fix it and note the correction.
