Airbnb's Brian Chesky Pledges $100 Million to San Francisco Parks, the City's Largest Single-Donor Gift

The money will speed up new parks, keep pools open longer and help finish waterfront projects, starting with five sites.

By the Happy News DeskNovember 9, 2026 · 2 min read

Photo: Frank Schulenburg / CC BY-SA 4.0 via Wikimedia Commons

Airbnb co-founder and CEO Brian Chesky is giving $100 million to San Francisco's public parks, Mayor Daniel Lurie announced on 9 November 2026. City officials describe it as the largest gift the city has ever received from a single donor.

Where the money goes

The gift will arrive in two phases. The first portion is earmarked for five projects:

  • Rachele Sullivan Park in SoMa, a new park ($39 million)
  • Jackson Playground renovations in Potrero Hill
  • Keeping the Garfield Pool in the Mission open on Sundays
  • Embarcadero park renovations
  • Completing India Basin Waterfront Park in Hunters Point

Reporting indicates the parks department will spend the next year deciding how to use the second half of the gift.

Why it matters

Parks department general manager Sarah Madland put the scale in context, saying the gift is about half of what the city typically gets in capital funding from a bond each decade. The previous largest private gift to city parks was $25 million from the John Pritzker Family Fund in 2019, for India Basin. The department has received roughly $300 million in donations over the past 15 years.

Context

The money is a pledge: the Board of Supervisors must approve incoming funds, and the first phase may arrive late this year or early in 2027. The department also faces budget pressures, so private gifts like this one help fill gaps that public funding may not cover.

Chesky, who founded Airbnb in San Francisco, has said he wanted to give back to the city. For residents, particularly in neighborhoods like the Mission and Hunters Point, the practical result should be more open pools, finished parks and new places to play.
