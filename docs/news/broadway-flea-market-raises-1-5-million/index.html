Broadway Flea Market Raises Nearly $1.5 Million for HIV/AIDS Relief Despite Stormy Weather

The 40th edition brought the event's all-time total to more than $22 million for Broadway Cares/Equity Fights AIDS.

By the Happy News DeskSeptember 29, 2026 · 2 min read

Photo: Jim.henderson / CC BY-SA 4.0 via Wikimedia Commons

Rain did not stop Broadway fans. The 40th Broadway Flea Market & Grand Auction, held September 27 at Manhattan's Hammerstein Ballroom, raised $1,487,927 for Broadway Cares/Equity Fights AIDS, Playbill reports.

Four decades of giving

The flea market, where theater lovers buy props, signed Playbills, costumes and meet-and-greets with stars, has run since 1987. Across its history it has now raised more than $22 million.

Broadway Cares/Equity Fights AIDS funds HIV/AIDS services, food and medical programs across the U.S., and the Entertainment Community Fund, which supports people working in the performing arts.

More giving this week

  • The producers of the TV series *Heated Rivalry* launched the Irina Foundation to support LGBTQIA2S+ and underserved communities through sports and the arts. Kate Spade New York is the inaugural donor, with Harry's also supporting it, The Hollywood Reporter says.
  • A live show by The Bulwark in Springfield, Ohio raised more than $400,000 for the city's Haitian community, according to Good Good Good.
