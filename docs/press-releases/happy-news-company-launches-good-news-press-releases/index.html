Happy News Company Launches Press Release Publishing for Organizations Sharing Good News

Companies, nonprofits and community groups can publish impact stories from $99, with 25% off for registered nonprofits.

Press release from Happy News CompanyOctober 8, 2026 · 2 min read
Happy News CompanyPress Release

SQUAMISH, British Columbia, October 8, 2026 —

Happy News Company today launched a press release service built for good news: charitable donations, sustainability milestones, community programs, volunteer drives, scholarships and impact reports.

Organizations can submit an announcement at HappyNewsCompany.com and have it reviewed, formatted and published on a permanent page, typically within one business day.

What's included

  • A permanent, search-indexable page on HappyNewsCompany.com
  • Professional formatting with headline, subheadline, dateline and boilerplate
  • Placement in the Press Releases feed and RSS feed
  • Optional homepage placement, newsletter inclusion and a professionally written release

Registered nonprofits and charities receive 25% off any package.

Clearly labeled

Every submitted release is labeled as a press release and kept separate from Happy News Company's own reporting, which covers real, sourced good news about health, the planet, wildlife, science and communities.

Availability

Packages and the submission form are available now at happynewscompany.com/submit/.
