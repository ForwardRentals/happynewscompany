Squamish Photographer Opens Library of Sea to Sky Stock Photos, Licensed Directly With No Subscription

Jeremy Allen of The Full Time Hobby has put hundreds of photos of Squamish, Whistler and British Columbia online for businesses to license direct from the photographer.

Press release from The Full Time HobbyOctober 21, 2026 · 2 min read

Photo: Jeremy Allen / The Full Time Hobby

SQUAMISH, British Columbia, October 21, 2026 —

Local businesses in the Sea to Sky corridor often end up using generic stock photos of mountains somewhere else. Squamish photographer Jeremy Allen wants to change that.

Allen, who shoots commercial photo and video as The Full Time Hobby, has opened a stock photo library of more than 450 images at thefulltimehobby.com/stock. It covers Squamish, Whistler, Pemberton, Garibaldi Park and the rest of British Columbia, plus selected shots from Alaska, the Canadian Rockies and the United States. Mountains, backcountry skiing, mountain biking, climbing, northern lights and West Coast sunsets are all there.

Simple, direct licensing

Every photo is licensed directly from the photographer, with no subscription and no middleman. Licences are royalty-free for the use the buyer describes:

  • Web & Social, $49: websites, blogs, newsletters and organic social media.
  • Commercial, $149: paid ads, marketing campaigns, and print runs up to 10,000 copies, with the full-resolution file.
  • Extended, by quote: products for resale, packaging, billboards, TV and exclusive use on select images.

Buyers open a photo, tap "License This Photo," describe how they'll use it, and receive an invoice and the full-resolution, unwatermarked file. The library can be filtered by location, category and colour.

Allen has shot for brands including Arc'teryx, Red Bull and Teton Gravity Research, and also rents out his own camera gear in Squamish and takes on custom photo and video work.

About The Full Time Hobby

The Full Time Hobby is the photography and film business of Jeremy Allen, a commercial photographer and filmmaker based in Squamish, British Columbia, focused on action, adventure and the outdoors.
