Oracle announced on 9 November 2026 a $1.55 million grant to Trinity Community Commons (TCC), an East Nashville nonprofit that is converting a historic church property into a neighborhood gathering place.

## What the grant pays for

The money will renovate TCC's community hub, which hosts free community meals, neighborhood events and connections to services.

- A reimagined hub with a new **dining room** where neighbors can share nutritious meals and meet service providers.
- **Free community events** meant to build connections between neighbors.

TCC says a groundbreaking date will be announced soon.

> "Trinity Community Commons provides a vital community gathering space for all," said Scott Twaddle, Oracle's senior vice president of development.

## The bigger project

TCC's wider redevelopment of the property also includes:

- **22 affordable and supportive homes** for families.
- A **teaching kitchen and garden** for healthier food access, workforce training and life skills.

The nonprofit describes its approach as "radical hospitality," welcoming people from all backgrounds, particularly those often left out of community life. It already runs weekly community dinners.

## Why it matters

Oracle is developing a large campus in Nashville, and this grant ties that corporate presence to a local nonprofit working on food, housing and belonging. Community spaces like this tend to depend on private gifts to get built.
