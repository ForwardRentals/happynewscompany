For nearly four decades, Gloria Kennard has cooked a free Thanksgiving meal for her neighbours in Bryan, Texas. This year, the community cooked up a surprise for her: a brand-new car.

## The surprise

Kennard's truck was totaled on July 29 as she drove to Bible study and choir rehearsal, and she had been relying on friends for rides. Her insurance did not cover a replacement, so friends at The Bridge Ministries, a food pantry where she volunteers, started a GoFundMe with a $15,000 goal.

- **Donors:** 174 people had given more than $17,700 by October
- **Business gift:** Kent Moore Cabinets contributed $10,000
- **Total:** more than $27,000, according to The Bridge Ministries
- **The car:** a 2027 Kia Seltos, presented at her home on Friday, October 30

> "I think every dollar represents a life that Miss Gloria has touched." — Emma Bridges, The Bridge Ministries

## A lifetime of feeding people

Kennard began her Thanksgiving meal in 1988. KBTX reports it now serves more than 2,000 people a year. She also hosts a New Year's Day meal and has volunteered at The Bridge Ministries pantry nearly every week for 16 years.

## Learning the new ride

One adjustment remains: Kennard has always used a key, so she has to get used to a push-button start. Her message to donors was simple.

> "Thank you so very much, community. And God bless you." — Gloria Kennard
