Charging an electric car at home overnight in the UK now costs a fraction of filling a tank, according to a new Carbon Brief analysis.

## Cost per mile

- **Diesel:** about 21p per mile
- **Petrol:** about 20.1p per mile
- **EV, off-peak home charging:** about 2.3p per mile
- **EV, peak-rate charging:** about 7p per mile

At off-peak rates, that is roughly nine times cheaper than petrol or diesel.

## Turkey's fast switch

Turkey shows how quickly the shift can happen. Electric cars went from about 1 in 100 new car sales in 2022 to about 1 in 5 today, according to [Our World in Data](https://ourworldindata.org/data-insights/electric-car-sales-are-growing-rapidly-in-turkey). A 2023 tax break, the domestic Togg brand and cheaper imports drove the growth.

## The bigger picture

WWF's new Living Planet Report notes that EVs made up more than a quarter of global new car sales in 2024, part of what its lead author called a "positive tipping point" in the energy system.
